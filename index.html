Our Approach

Downside first.
Upside by design.

We are investors and operators. Every position begins with a simple question: what is this worth if things don’t go to plan?

We seek to acquire land, projects, and businesses at prices supported by tangible downside value—the underlying land, the replacement cost of improvements, or durable operating cash flow. From that foundation, we pursue upside through the work we control: securing entitlements, building, and improving operations.

Returns are realized through the path that best serves the asset and our partners—an outright sale, a refinancing, a recapitalization, or ongoing cash distributions.

  1. 01

    Acquire

    At prices anchored to downside value—land basis, replacement cost, or proven cash flow.

  2. 02

    Enhance

    Through entitlements and approvals, ground-up construction, and hands-on operating improvements.

  3. 03

    Realize

    Via sale, refinancing, recapitalization, or durable ongoing cash flow to partners.

Investment Strategies

Four disciplines.
One underwriting standard.

Our focus spans real assets and operating businesses, unified by a consistent discipline: buy well, add value directly, and exit with intent.

Strategy I

Land Development & Entitlements

We acquire well-located land and unlock value through the entitlement process—zoning, site-plan approvals, and permitting. Entitled land is a fundamentally different asset than raw acreage, and the approvals themselves are a source of value creation.

  • Rezoning & land-use approvals
  • Site planning & horizontal readiness
  • Exit to builders, developers, or self-development

Strategy II

Ground-Up Industrial Flex

We develop modern flex space serving small and mid-sized businesses—contractors, light manufacturers, distributors, and service firms that need functional warehouse space combined with office and showroom.

  • Build-to-suit & speculative development
  • Multi-tenant, diversified rent rolls
  • Stabilize, then hold, refinance, or sell

Strategy III

New & Established Businesses

We make selective investments in private companies—acquiring control or meaningful equity stakes—and work alongside management to strengthen operations, sharpen capital allocation, and build durable cash flow.

  • Control acquisitions & minority stakes
  • Operational & financial improvement
  • Growth capital for proven models

Strategy IV

Opportunistic Alternatives

We pursue opportunistic investments in art, collectibles, and other alternative assets where specialized knowledge, sourcing, and patience can create value that broader markets overlook.

  • Fine art & collectibles
  • Special situations
  • Asymmetric, low-correlation exposure

Value Creation

How we turn a sound basis
into a superior outcome.

Phase 01

Source & Underwrite

Proprietary and relationship-driven sourcing. Every opportunity is underwritten first to its downside: land value, replacement cost, liquidation value, or in-place cash flow.

Phase 02

Acquire with Discipline

We structure acquisitions to protect capital—appropriate leverage, contingencies, and terms that reflect the risks we are taking.

Phase 03

Create Value

We execute the business plan directly: pursuing approvals, managing construction, leasing space, and working inside operating companies to improve performance.

Phase 04

Realize Returns

We choose the exit that best serves the asset and our partners—a sale, refinancing, recapitalization, or long-term hold for cash flow.

Approvals

Converting land into entitled, development-ready sites.

Construction

Delivering new, in-demand space at a cost basis below market value.

Operations

Improving margins, management, and capital efficiency.

Investment Philosophy — NB Holdings Partnership

Guiding Principles

The commitments behind
every investment.

I.

Capital Preservation

We underwrite to the downside before we consider the upside. A margin of safety at entry is the foundation of every position.

II.

Sponsor Alignment

As a sponsor-led firm, we invest alongside our partners. Our interests are aligned with theirs from acquisition through realization.

III.

Operator Mindset

We don’t simply allocate capital—we do the work. Entitlements, construction, and operations are where we create value directly.

IV.

Disciplined Realization

We define the path to liquidity before we invest and remain flexible in how we get there—sale, refinance, recap, or cash flow.

V.

Transparency

Clear, consistent communication with our investors. We report the good and the challenging with equal candor.

VI.

Long-Term Relationships

We seek partners for the long term—investors, lenders, municipalities, and operators who return to work with us again.

Investor Relations

Partner with us.

We work with accredited investors, family offices, and institutional partners who share our long-term, value-oriented perspective.

  • Who We Work With

    Accredited investors as defined under Rule 501 of Regulation D, family offices, registered investment advisors, and institutional allocators.

  • How to Participate

    We sponsor offerings under both Rule 506(c) and Rule 506(b) of Regulation D, on a fund or deal-by-deal basis. Rule 506(c) offerings are open to verified accredited investors. Rule 506(b) offerings are made only privately, to investors with whom we have an established, substantive relationship. Submit an inquiry and our Investor Relations team will follow up to discuss your objectives.

  • What to Expect

    Following an introductory conversation, qualified investors may receive offering materials for current and upcoming opportunities. Participation in Rule 506(c) offerings requires verification of accredited status, such as a letter from a CPA, attorney, or registered investment adviser.
